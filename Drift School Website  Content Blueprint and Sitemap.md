## 2. Positioning and Messaging Pillars

**Tagline options**
- "Control is the coolest skill."
- "Learn the slide. Master the road."
- "Where car control is taught, not tried."
- "Kenya's safe start in motorsport."

**Five messaging pillars (use across every page)**

1. **Safety by design.** Closed course, low-power cars, instructor-controlled sessions, safeguarding officer on site.
2. **Real education.** Physics of grip, vehicle dynamics, mechanics and sports science, mapped to the CBC/CBE Senior School pathways.
3. **Road-safety payoff.** Learning what a car does at the limit, in a controlled place, so learners recognise and avoid it on public roads.
4. **A pathway, not a joyride.** Structured levels, assessments, certificates, and routes into karting, autocross and licensed motorsport.
5. **Belonging.** Crew culture, teamwork, pit roles and media roles for learners who don't want to drive.

**Proof points to collect before launch:** instructor CVs and licences, insurance certificate, venue risk assessment, safeguarding policy PDF, first-aid/medic arrangement, 2–3 parent testimonials from pilot sessions, one principal reference letter.

## 6. FIA-Aligned Safeguarding Framework

### 6.1 What "FIA-aligned" can and cannot mean

The FIA's Safeguarding Policy and Regulations (Appendix S to the International Sporting Code) were approved by the World Motor Sport Council, took effect in October 2024 for FIA World Championships and FIA-sanctioned meetings, and apply **across all other FIA events from 2026** ([FIA Activity Report 2024](https://activityreport2024.fia.com/sport-championships/sporting/)). The FIA encourages each national sporting authority (ASN) to adopt its own safeguarding policy and gives them templates and guidelines to do so ([FIA ASN Hub](https://www.fia.com/asn-hub)).

**Recommended website wording:**
> "Our Safeguarding Policy is modelled on the principles of the FIA Safeguarding Policy ('Driven by Respect') and the safeguarding requirements of Kenya's national motorsport authority."

**Do not** say "FIA-certified", "FIA-approved" or "FIA-accredited" unless you hold that status in writing. The nearest formal FIA framework for young drivers is the **Young Driver Safety Programme**. Under it, ASNs (not private schools) can be assessed against three levels of training standard ([FIA Young Driver Safety Programme](https://api.fia.com/young-driver-safety-programme)). The realistic route is to partner with the ASN's youth development program.

**Kenya's national body: check this before publishing.** Kenya's Sports Disputes Tribunal ordered the registration of Motorsports Kenya and found that the old Kenya Motor Sports Federation (KMSF) no longer had legal recognition ([Citizen Digital](https://citizen.digital/article/sdt-declares-motorsports-kenya-as-legitimate-body-for-the-sport-n367661)). Motorsports Kenya Federation was then formally registered by the Sports Registrar ([KBC](https://www.kbc.co.ke/sigh-of-relief-as-motorsports-kenya-federation-is-registered/)) and has elected Altaf Ganatra as its first president ([KBC](https://www.kbc.co.ke/ganatra-elected-president-as-motorsport-kenya-federation-new-era-dawns/)). KMSF's website still describes itself as the FIA-recognised ASN ([KMSF](https://motorsportkenya.com/about-us)). Before you name any body on your site, confirm in writing which one the FIA currently recognises and get your venue or events sanctioned by that body.

### 6.2 The four principles (use as a page section)

The FIA policy rests on four principles ([FIA – Driven by Respect](https://www.fia.com/driven-by-respect)). Below is how to present each one on your site:

| FIA principle | [Academy] commitment (copy) |
|---|---|
| **Welfare of the individual** | "A learner's safety and wellbeing come before any lap, any lesson or any photo." |
| **Equality and inclusion** | "Every learner is welcome, whatever their gender, background, disability, faith or ability to pay. Bursaries keep the door open." |
| **Accountability** | "Every adult at [Academy] is responsible for acting on a concern, and we publish how we handle them." |
| **Confidentiality** | "Concerns are shared only with people who need to know in order to protect a learner." |

### 6.3 Prohibited conduct (policy page section)

The FIA names five forms of harassment and abuse: psychological abuse (including hazing), physical abuse (including training loads that are inappropriate for a learner's age or body, or training while injured), sexual harassment, sexual abuse, and neglect. Bullying, hazing and grooming are treated the same way, whether in person or online ([FIA – Which conducts are prohibited?](https://www.fia.com/which-conducts-are-prohibited)).

Add motorsport-specific rules to your code of conduct:
- No "initiation" rituals for new learners or crew (hazing)
- No pushing a learner to drive faster, longer or while unwell, upset or injured
- No private messaging between staff and learners. All communication goes through group channels that include parents or the school
- No transporting a single learner in a staff vehicle without written parental consent and a second adult
- No photos or video of learners on personal staff phones

### 6.4 Roles and people

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL)** | Owns the policy, receives concerns, links with the Directorate of Children Services, police and the ASN |
| **Deputy DSL** | Present at every session when the DSL is away |
| **Instructors** | Hold motorsport licence/qualification, first aid, safeguarding training |
| **Marshals** | Course safety, flag signals, emergency stop |
| **School-appointed teacher** | Accompanies school groups. The Ministry of Education's safety manual requires written parental consent and an accompanying teacher for excursions ([MoE Safety Standards Manual](https://aak.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2008-Safety-Standards-Manual-for-Schools_Kenya.pdf)) |

### 6.5 Safer recruitment (publish as "How we vet our instructors")

Modelled on international sport safeguarding toolkits, which call for safeguarding in role descriptions and interviews, criminal record checks where available, reference checks, safeguarding induction training and a signed code of conduct for anyone working with children ([FIFA Guardians Toolkit](https://www.sporthumanrights.org/media/u5nhkdhw/toolkit-fifa-guardians.pdf)):

1. Safeguarding questions in every interview
2. Police Clearance Certificate (Certificate of Good Conduct) from the DCI, renewed every year
3. Two references, including one from a role with young people
4. Safeguarding induction plus yearly refresher
5. Signed code of conduct
6. Probation period with observed sessions

### 6.6 Supervision ratios

The same toolkit recommends at least one adult per 20 children for ages 9–18, with a minimum of two adults at every activity ([FIFA Guardians Toolkit](https://www.sporthumanrights.org/media/u5nhkdhw/toolkit-fifa-guardians.pdf)). Motorsport carries more risk, so set much tighter ratios and publish them:

| Activity | Recommended ratio |
|---|---|
| In-car driving | 1 instructor : 1 learner (instructor in car or on live radio with kill-switch) |
| Skid pad / course zone | 1 marshal per active car zone + 1 supervising instructor |
| Classroom / simulator | 1 adult : 10 learners, minimum 2 adults |
| Pit / Crew Track | 1 adult : 6 learners (tools, jacks, hot parts) |
| Breaks and lunch | DSL or deputy + accompanying teacher |

### 6.7 Reporting a concern (page and flow)

- Short on-page form (it can be anonymous), WhatsApp line, and DSL phone/email
- What happens next: acknowledgement within 24 hrs → risk assessment → referral where needed → outcome logged
- Escalation contacts: national child helpline (Childline Kenya, 116), police, Directorate of Children Services, the ASN
- Also link to the FIA Ethics & Compliance Hotline. It has a safeguarding section, is open 24/7 and accepts anonymous reports ([FIA – Report a prohibited conduct](https://www.fia.com/report-prohibited-conduct))

### 6.8 Children's data protection (Kenya)

Kenya's Data Protection Act, Section 33, bans processing a child's personal data unless a parent or guardian consents and the processing protects the child's best interests. It also requires age verification and consent mechanisms ([Kenya Law – Data Protection Act](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24/eng@2022-12-31)). The Data Commissioner's guidance treats anyone under 18 as a child and calls for verifiable parental consent before you collect any child's data ([ODPC Guidance Note](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/ODPC-%E2%80%93-Guidance-Note-for-Processing-Childrens-Data.pdf)). The General Regulations also ban profiling children for direct marketing ([Data Protection (General) Regulations](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2021/263/eng@2022-12-31)).

**What this means for the website:**
- Students under 18 **cannot** complete a booking alone. The student form only collects a first name and the parent's phone or email, then hands off to the parent (see Flow A).
- Keep separate consent tick-boxes for: participation · medical data · photos for internal use · photos for marketing · WhatsApp updates
- Don't retarget under-18s with ads. Target retargeting at parents and schools.
- Register as a data controller/processor with the ODPC if your size or activity requires it.

### 6.9 Track, car and kit standards (Safety > Standards page)

- Closed venue with run-off areas, barriers and no spectators in the car zone
- Low-power rear-wheel-drive cars, inspected before each session, with a pre-session checklist on file
- Speed caps per level (e.g. Level 1: second gear, under ~40 km/h on a wet pad, subject to your risk assessment)
- Helmet (current standard), neck support where suitable, fire-resistant overalls, gloves, closed shoes
- Fire extinguishers at the car and the pit, a trained first-aider, an agreed ambulance/hospital route
- Weather and fatigue rules (stop sessions in heat, poor visibility or when a learner is tired)
- Written incident and near-miss log, reviewed every month

## 7. Beginner Drift Curriculum

### 7.1 Age-tiered access (check with insurer and ASN)

| Tier | Age | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| **Sim & Theory Track** | 13–15 | Simulator, vehicle dynamics, mechanics, marshal basics, passenger "hot laps" only if the insurer allows |
| **Level 1 – Foundations** | 16–19 | Supervised driving on a wet skid pad in prepared cars |
| **Level 2 – Linking** | 16–19, after passing Level 1 | Figure-8s, transitions, simple courses |
| **Crew Track** | 13–19 | Pit crew, mechanics, data, media; no driving needed |

### 7.2 Level 1 – Beginner Drift Foundations (6 sessions × 3 hrs)

| Session | Theme | Classroom (45 min) | Practical (2 hrs) | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Safety & the car** | Rules, kit, cockpit | Code of conduct, flags, radio calls, emergency stop; parts of a RWD car | Seating position, steering grip, smooth throttle and braking in a straight line | Safety quiz (100% required) |
| **2. Grip & weight transfer** | Physics of traction | Friction circle, weight transfer under braking and acceleration | Slalom, braking points, feeling the car's weight shift | Instructor checklist |
| **3. Understeer vs oversteer** | Losing and regaining grip | Slip angle, why cars spin, how to recover | Wet circle: feel oversteer at low speed, practise counter-steer recovery | Recovery drill: 3 clean recoveries |
| **4. Initiating a slide** | Controlled entry | Initiation methods (power-over, weight-transfer); why we avoid aggressive methods at this level | Static circle donuts with instructor coaching | Hold a slide for a quarter circle |
| **5. Holding the slide** | Throttle and steering balance | Looking where you want to go; throttle modulation | Constant-radius drifts both ways | Hold a half-circle each direction |
| **6. Review & road transfer** | Bringing it home | Road-safety debrief: skids on wet tarmac and murram, ABS/ESC, speed choice | Graded run + peer coaching | Level 1 Certificate + badge |

**Every session includes:** a pre-session briefing, a car check done by the learner with the instructor, a fatigue and wellbeing check-in, a video debrief and a reflection journal entry.

### 7.3 Level 2 – Linking & Control (6 sessions, outline)

Figure-8 transitions · Clipping points and lines · Handbrake theory (demo only, then supervised) · Simple tandem following at a safe distance (instructor-led) · Data logging and video analysis · Final showcase in front of parents.

### 7.4 Crew Track (runs alongside the driving levels)

Tyre changes and torque · Fluids and pre-session checks · Basic suspension and alignment · Timing and data · Marshal and flag duties · Filming and editing motorsport content · Event day operations.

### 7.5 CBC/CBE Senior School alignment (for the Schools > Curriculum page)

Kenya's Senior School has three pathways: STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts and Sports Science ([CBC Edu Kenya](https://cbcedukenya.com/blog/grade-10-pathway-selection-kenya-2026)). STEM technical electives include Power Mechanics, Electrical Technology, Metal Technology and Drawing and Design. The Sports Science cluster includes Sports and Recreation, Sports Coaching and Officiating, and Sports Management ([CBC Edu Kenya – KICD designs](https://cbcedukenya.com/blog/kicd-grade-10-senior-school-curriculum-designs-pathways)). Community Service Learning is part of the compulsory core ([CBC Edu Kenya](https://cbcedukenya.com/blog/grade-10-pathway-selection-kenya-2026)).

| [Academy] module | Pathway / subject link | Example learning outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Grip & weight transfer | STEM – Physics | Explain friction and Newton's laws using measured data from the car |
| Car checks & Crew Track | STEM – Power Mechanics, Electrical Technology | Carry out a safe pre-use check of a vehicle and explain each system |
| Data & video analysis | STEM – Computer Studies, Mathematics | Graph speed and angle data and draw conclusions |
| Mental preparation & fatigue | Arts & Sports Science – Sports Science / PE | Use a pre-performance routine and recovery strategies |
| Marshal & event operations | Sports Coaching & Officiating, Sports Management | Officiate a session using flag protocols |
| Motorsport media | Arts – Media / Theatre & Film / Photography | Produce a 60-second safety-messaging video |
| Road-safety outreach | Community Service Learning | Run a road-safety awareness session for juniors or the community |

Map each module to the exact KICD strand and sub-strand codes before you publish. The curriculum designs are on the KICD website.

## 9. Conversion-Optimised CTA User Flows

### 9.1 CTA hierarchy

| Intent | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA | Low-commitment CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student | `Book a Taster` (hands off to parent) | `Send this to my parent` | `Join the waitlist` |
| Parent | `Book a Taster` | `Chat on WhatsApp` | `Download the Parent Guide` |
| School | `Book a School Briefing` | `Download Prospectus` | `Invite us to a parents' meeting` |
| Sponsor | `Sponsor a Student` | `Request the impact report` | `Subscribe to updates` |

### Flow A: Student → Parent handoff (core B2C flow)

```
Student lands (TikTok/IG/school visit QR)
   → Program page (video-first)
   → CTA "I want in"
   → Mini-form: first name, age band, school, parent's phone/email
        (no other child data collected; DPA s.33)
   → "Done. We've sent your parent a link." + share button:
        prefilled WhatsApp to parent: "Hi, I want to do the [Academy]
        Level 1 course. Here's the safety info and booking link: [link]"
   → Parent receives SMS/WhatsApp/email with a secure link
   → Parent: Safety page summary → select date → consent forms →
     medical info → photo consent options → M-Pesa STK push payment
   → Confirmation (SMS + email + calendar file) + "What to bring" checklist
   → Reminders: 72 hrs, 24 hrs (weather/kit), morning-of
   → Post-session: certificate, photos (if consented), feedback survey,
     Level 2 upsell, referral code
```

**Why it converts:** the teen supplies the motivation and the parent supplies the consent and payment. The handoff message is pre-written, so the teen does the "selling."

### Flow B: Parent direct booking

1. Landing (Facebook/Google/referral) → Parents hub
2. Video "A day at [Academy]" → Safety snapshot
3. `Book a Taster` → choose date (live seat count)
4. Step 1/4 Learner details → Step 2/4 Consent & medical → Step 3/4 Photo/media choices → Step 4/4 Payment (M-Pesa, card, or "pay in 3")
5. Progress bar on screen, autosave, and a "Save and finish later" link sent to WhatsApp
6. Abandoned-booking recovery: WhatsApp nudge after 2 hrs, email after 24 hrs ("Questions? Our safety lead can call you.")

### Flow C: School partnership

```
Principal/teacher lands (LinkedIn, email outreach, referral, Google "school trips Nakuru")
   → For Schools hub
   → Option 1: "Download Prospectus" (gate: name, role, school, school email, phone)
        → Auto-email + 5-step nurture (8.5)
   → Option 2: "Book a 30-min Briefing" (calendar, choose: on-site / Zoom / at venue)
        → Pre-briefing form: pathways offered, learner numbers, preferred term
        → Personalised deck sent 24 hrs before
   → Briefing → Pilot Discovery Day proposal
   → Parent consent links issued per learner (bulk link + class roster)
   → Teacher dashboard: consent status per learner, payment status, trip-approval letter download
   → Pilot delivered → post-visit report → MoU signing → Term Program
```

### Flow D: Group consent for school bookings

- School gets one class link and parents each complete their child's consent and pay (or the school pays)
- Teacher dashboard shows ✅ consented / ⏳ pending / ❌ declined
- Automatic reminders to pending parents at 7, 3 and 1 days before the visit
- Learners without full consent take part only in the classroom/simulator stations, never in the cars

### Flow E: Report a concern (must be friction-free)

Footer link on every page → single page → three options (online form, WhatsApp, phone) → optional anonymity → instant acknowledgement → link to external helplines.

### 9.2 Conversion best practices

- **Mobile first:** most Kenyan traffic is mobile. Keep pages under 2 seconds on 3G, compress video and use poster images.
- **WhatsApp everywhere:** click-to-chat with prefilled messages for each page ("Hi, I'm asking about the Holiday Camp…")
- **M-Pesa STK push** at checkout, plus Paybill/Till shown as a fallback
- **Scarcity and urgency only when true:** live seat counts, real cohort start dates
- **Trust beside every CTA:** a one-line safety reassurance under each booking button
- **Sticky mobile CTA bar:** `Book` · `WhatsApp` · `Call`
- **Exit intent (desktop) / scroll-depth (mobile):** offer the Parent Guide PDF
- **Referral loop:** each completed learner gets a code, and both the learner and the friend get a discount
